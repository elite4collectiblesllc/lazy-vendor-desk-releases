# Elite 4 Lazy Vendor Desk Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

We collect only what Elite 4 Lazy Vendor Desk needs to keep your shop's inventory and transactions in step across your devices. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Elite 4 Collectibles LLC ("we") makes Elite 4 Lazy Vendor Desk: the desktop program for Windows and the E4 L.V.D. phone app for Android, iPhone and iPad. This policy covers both, including beta versions shared through TestFlight.

## What we collect

- **Your sign-in.** Your email address and password. Google Firebase Authentication keeps them; we never see your password.
- **Your shop's data**, when you use the phone app or connect the desktop program to the cloud: the shop's name, its inventory items (descriptions, condition or grade, certification numbers, quantities, values, costs, dates and locations), its transactions (what came in and went out, cash, location and notes), its settings (replacement rules, the Google Sheet connection, the card scanner key), the Deckbox list and labels waiting to print.
- **Your shop's sign-ins.** The email address, name and role (owner or staff) of each person an owner adds.
- **Device names.** A computer's name (such as SHOP-PC) or a phone's sign-in email, shown to the shop's other devices: which computer keeps the Inventory Manager file, who is comping an item, where a transaction was saved.
- **Problem reports**, only when you send one with Report a Problem: what you wrote, the app and its version, your device model and system version, the screen you were on, your shop's name and ID, the app's recent error messages, a screenshot if you include one, and your email address if you leave that box checked.
- **Card photos**, only when a shop turns on Scan Cards: each photo goes to Ximilar to recognize the cards. We don't store it.

Barcode scanning happens on your device. The apps don't collect your location, contacts or browsing history, and they contain no advertising or analytics tools.

## How we use it

- **To run the apps:** signing you in, keeping your shop's data the same on its phones and computers, sending transactions to your shop's Google Sheet, and passing labels to the computer with the label printer.
- **To fix problems:** reading problem reports, finding the cause and testing fixes.
- **To help you** when you ask us for support.

We don't use your information for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

## Who it's shared with

Your shop's data is visible only to the sign-ins of your shop; other shops using the apps can't see it. We share information only with these services, and only for what the table says:

| Service | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Firebase (Google) | Sign-ins, your shop's data, problem reports | Stores sign-ins and the shop's cloud data |
| GitHub | Problem reports, copied to our private repository; your device's internet address when the apps check for updates | Tracks reports; hosts the app updates |
| Anthropic (Claude, an AI assistant) | Problem reports | Helps us review reports and prepare fixes |
| Ximilar | Card photos, only for shops that turn on Scan Cards with their own Ximilar account | Recognizes the cards |
| Your shop's own Google Sheet | The shop's transactions and inventory rows, only if the shop connects a sheet | Keeps your sheet up to date; it's in your Google account |
| Apple (TestFlight, App Store) | Crash reports and feedback, only if you choose to send them through Apple | Beta testing and distribution, under Apple's privacy policy |

As the operator of the cloud service, we can technically access the shop data stored there. We do so only to give support you ask for, keep the service running and secure, or when the law requires it.

## Where it's kept and for how long

- **Cloud data** is stored with Google Firebase for as long as your shop and sign-in exist. It's deleted when you delete your account (below) or ask us to delete it.
- **Problem reports** are kept in our private GitHub repository while they help us fix problems. A report's screenshot is removed from the cloud once it's copied there. Ask us and we'll delete your reports.
- **On your computer**, the desktop program keeps its Inventory Manager file, transaction history and settings on that computer, and in any sync folder you choose (such as Google Drive). We don't receive these unless you connect the program to the cloud.
- **On your phone**, the app keeps a copy of your shop's data so it works without a connection. Deleting the app removes it.

## Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account yourself in the phone app: **Settings → Delete My Account**. It needs your password and an internet connection.

- **Staff, or an owner while another owner stays:** your sign-in is deleted and you're taken off the shop. The shop's data stays with the shop.
- **A shop's only owner:** you can first save a copy of the shop's inventory and transactions as spreadsheet files. Then the shop's data is deleted from the cloud, on every phone and computer. Computers keep their own Inventory Manager file and history.

You can also email us (below) to delete your account, your shop's data or your problem reports.

## Security

Everything the apps send travels encrypted (HTTPS). The cloud's security rules let only a shop's own sign-ins read or change its data, and only its owners add or remove sign-ins. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and never stored by us. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and remove sign-ins that are no longer needed.

## Children

The apps are business tools for card shops and vendors and aren't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and correct** your shop's data in the apps at any time.
- **Take a copy:** the desktop program keeps your inventory as an Excel file, and Delete My Account offers a copy before a shop is deleted.
- **Delete** your account and data as described above.
- **Problem reports are optional.** Uncheck the email box or leave out the screenshot if you prefer.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to request a copy of your information or limit its use. Contact us to use them.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app or by email first.
